The Reality

People problems are operations problems in disguise

Ask any plant owner what keeps them up at night and machines rarely come first. It's the skilled operator who left for a competitor. The supervisor whose exit took twenty years of process knowledge with it. The incentive dispute that soured a whole shift. None of this shows up on a production dashboard—but all of it shows up in your output.

📒 Attendance and shifts run on registers

Biometric machines that don't talk to payroll. Registers that get "adjusted." Shift swaps agreed verbally. By month-end, reconciling who actually worked what becomes a two-day argument nobody wins.

🧮 Incentives are opaque—and everyone knows it

When workers can't see how their incentive was calculated, they assume the worst. Opacity breeds disputes, disputes breed distrust, and distrust walks out the gate to the factory next door offering the same wage.

📉 Performance lives in the owner's memory

Who's your best machinist, objectively? Who's improving, who's coasting? Without records, promotions feel arbitrary, good people feel invisible, and the loudest voice—not the best worker—gets the raise.

🎓 Skills exist, but no one can see them

Which operators are certified on which machines? What happens when your only die-setter is on leave? A skills matrix in someone's head is a production risk waiting for a bad day.

🚪 Attrition is discovered, never predicted

The resignation always "comes out of nowhere." Except it never does—the signals were there in attendance patterns, disputes, sidelined performance. Nobody was looking, because nothing was recorded.

📋 Compliance paperwork eats real time

PF, ESI, contractor records, audit trails—assembled by hand, every time, from scattered sources. It gets done, but it costs your office team days that should go to running the business.

In Development

End to end means hire to retire

One connected system for the whole employee journey—built for factory floors, not corporate offices. Designed around how developing-market plants actually work: shifts, contractors, incentive-linked pay—and vernacular-first interfaces, starting with Hindi and Punjabi.

1 · Hire & onboard

Structured hiring for operator, supervisor, and staff roles—clear job definitions, candidate records, and an onboarding checklist so new joiners are productive in days, not months. AllAboutHR's recruitment expertise built into the workflow.

2 · Attendance, shifts & leave

One source of truth connecting biometric data, shift rosters, overtime, and leave—feeding payroll inputs automatically. Month-end reconciliation becomes a report you read, not a fight you referee.

3 · Performance & incentives

The heart of the system. Role-wise KPIs tied to real production data, transparent incentive calculations every worker can see and verify, and structured reviews that make promotions defensible and recognition visible.

4 · Skills matrix & training

Who is certified on what, at a glance. Training records, certification expiries, and coverage gaps flagged before they become production stoppages—so one person on leave never halts a line.

5 · Retention signals

Patterns surfaced from the data you're already capturing—attendance dips, dispute frequency, stalled growth—so the conversation with a valued worker happens before the resignation letter, not after.

6 · Compliance-ready records

Employee records structured so PF, ESI, contractor, and audit documentation assembles from the system instead of from memory. When the inspector visits, you print—you don't scramble.

Module scope reflects what is currently in development and may evolve as early partner factories shape the build. AI-assisted capabilities are on the roadmap and will be introduced in phases, only after the underlying data foundation is proven at each plant.

The Alliance

Software alone doesn't fix people problems. Neither does advice alone.

HR software vendors sell you a login and disappear. HR consultants write you a policy and disappear. We think the answer is both disciplines working the same engagement—the system and the practice, built together, embedded in your plant.

🖥️ Kinexus — the digital system

The data backbone and workflows: attendance and shift integration, performance dashboards tied to production data, transparent incentive engines, skills matrices, and records that generate compliance paperwork. Built on the same embedded model as everything we do—we stay until your team runs it.

🤝 AllAboutHR — the people practice

The HR expertise the system encodes: role definitions and KPI design, incentive structures that motivate rather than antagonize, review processes supervisors will actually follow, hiring support, and the policy foundations that keep it all compliant and fair.
