Built for FMCG & Packaging

A Crawl-Walk-Run-Fly path tailored to your shop floor

We typically start with artwork/spec version control and SKU inventory — the two areas most likely to cause costly production errors.

Artwork & Spec Version Control System Standard Module

A simple digital system ensuring production always references the latest approved artwork and packaging specification for each brand owner and SKU.

  • Reduced risk of producing against outdated specs
  • Clear audit trail of spec approvals and changes
  • Fewer costly rework/repack incidents
High-SKU Inventory & Planning Tool Standard Module

A structured inventory and planning system designed for high SKU counts, covering raw material, packaging material and finished goods by brand and pack size.

  • Reduced stock-outs on fast-moving SKUs
  • Lower packaging material waste
  • Better-informed production planning
Digital Batch Quality & Compliance Records Custom Build

Converts batch quality checks (weights, fill levels, codes, compliance parameters) into structured digital records for fast retrieval during brand-owner audits.

  • Faster brand-owner and regulatory audits
  • Consistent, searchable quality records
  • Reduced audit-prep workload
Agentic Changeover & Line Efficiency Analyst Custom Build

An AI agent that tracks changeover times and line efficiency across SKUs and shifts, identifying opportunities to reduce lost production time.

  • Changeover time reductions identified and tracked
  • Improved overall line efficiency (OEE)
  • Data-backed conversations with brand owners on capacity
