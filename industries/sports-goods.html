Where We See Sports Goods Manufacturers Stuck

The operational gaps quietly eating your margins

Seasonality and specification complexity create operational whiplash that most units absorb through overtime and firefighting.

📅

Seasonal Demand Whiplash

Production swings hard between peak and lean seasons, but planning is still done order-by-order without a forward capacity view.

🧵

Multi-Component Order Complexity

A single order (e.g. protective gear, balls, kits) often involves multiple sub-components and finishing steps tracked across different registers.

✉️

Buyer Specification Changes

Brand buyers frequently revise specs mid-order — tracking which version of a spec each batch was made to is largely manual.

📦

Sampling & Approval Delays

Sample development for new seasons competes with live production for the same resources, with no separate tracking lane.

🚚

Shipment Consolidation Complexity

Orders for the same buyer often ship in multiple lots from different production lines, making consolidated dispatch planning difficult.

📊

Limited Visibility into True Margins

With multiple components and frequent spec changes, true order-level profitability is rarely calculated until well after dispatch.

Built for Sports Goods

A Crawl-Walk-Run-Fly path tailored to your shop floor

We focus on giving you a forward view of capacity and order status — the two things that matter most when buyers expect speed and flexibility.

Seasonal Capacity & Order Planning Board Standard Module

A simple planning system that maps incoming orders against available capacity by season, helping you commit to realistic delivery dates.

  • Fewer overtime fire-drills during peak season
  • Better-informed delivery commitments to buyers
  • Early visibility into lean-season capacity gaps
Multi-Component Order Tracker Standard Module

Tracks every component and finishing step of complex orders in one place, so nothing falls through the cracks between sub-assemblies.

  • Reduced missed components at final assembly
  • Clear status visibility for sales teams
  • Faster identification of late sub-components
Agentic Spec & Change Tracking Assistant Custom Build

An AI agent that logs buyer specification revisions against orders and flags which in-progress batches may be affected by a change.

  • Fewer costly rework cycles from missed spec updates
  • Clear audit trail of what was made to what spec
  • Faster buyer communication on change impact
Order-Level Margin Dashboard Custom Build

Connects order costs (materials, components, labor) with final pricing to show true margin per order, season and buyer.

  • Visibility into which orders/buyers are actually profitable
  • Better-informed pricing for future seasons
  • Data to support buyer price negotiations
