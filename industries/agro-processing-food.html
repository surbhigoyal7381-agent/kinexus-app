The operational gaps quietly eating your margins

Raw Material Source Traceability

Manual Quality & Safety Checks

???

Storage & Cold-Chain Logging

??

Export Certification Documentation

No Yield & Recovery Analysis

Built for Agro Processing & Food

A Crawl-Walk-Run-Fly path tailored to your shop floor

We prioritize traceability and quality documentation first — these directly unlock new buyers and reduce audit stress.

Source-to-Batch Traceability System Standard Module

Links incoming raw material lots to processing batches and finished goods, creating a digital trail from farm/supplier to dispatch.

  • Faster, more confident responses to buyer traceability queries
  • Stronger position for FSSAI and export audits
  • Foundation for future certification (e.g. organic, export-grade)
Digital Quality & Safety Logs Standard Module

Converts incoming, in-process and finished goods QC checks — plus storage condition logs — into structured digital records with alerts for out-of-range readings.

  • Audit-ready quality records at all times
  • Early alerts on storage condition deviations
  • Reduced paperwork burden on QC staff
FIFO/FEFO Inventory & Shelf-Life Tracker Custom Build

Tracks batch-wise inventory with expiry/shelf-life flags, helping ensure older stock moves first and near-expiry stock is flagged proactively.

  • Reduced near-expiry write-offs
  • Better stock rotation discipline
  • Clearer view of finished goods inventory by age
Agentic Export Documentation & Yield Analyst Custom Build

An AI agent that drafts export certification paperwork from your digital records and separately analyzes processing yield trends against raw material quality data.

  • Faster export documentation turnaround
  • Yield trends visible by lot, season and supplier
  • Data to support raw material sourcing decisions
