Why a loop and not a menu

Most firms present a menu. A menu invites you to buy one thing and leave.

This closes instead. Lens finds it. Blueprint and Calibrate fix it. The Alvora platforms instrument it. Proof measures the delta twelve months on — against a locked baseline, using identical instruments. Then the proof becomes the next diagnosis.

Consulting and software are usually two companies pretending to be one. Here they are the same business read twice: the loop the services run is the same loop the platform runs — evidence in, decision out, outcome measured, evidence back in.

Where clients come in

Three doors. All of them lead to the same loop.

Top-down

The MD buys Lens

Highest value, opens the whole house, hardest to sell cold. Everything else follows from what the diagnostic finds.

Side-door

The HR head buys Signals or Assure

Fast, quantitative, low friction — and the best-converting door into the loop.

Transactional

A vacancy brings in Search or Flow

Lowest value alone. Qualify hard, then walk it up the loop rather than sitting in it.

Packages

Six packs across the micro-to-enterprise journey.

For each one: who it's for, the services it includes, and the signal that the next pack has become the right one. Nothing changes as you grow except the depth — that is what a client is buying when they stay in a pack and simply get bigger.

Up to 10 employees

Comply Essentials

An owner with a handful of people, no HR, and a growing worry about what they are legally supposed to be doing.

Legally sound, fully documented, and off your desk. Nothing more — because nothing more is needed yet.

  • Statutory compliance calendar with filing reminders
  • POSH policy and Internal Committee guidance
  • Registration guidance — EPF, ESIC, S&E, PT
  • Contract and letter templates, core policy pack
  • Payroll and statutory deduction check
  • Alvora HRMS Essentials + one quarterly review call

When to move on: at 11 people this pack closes. Move to Comply — the policy set becomes yours rather than a template.

11 – 500 employees

Comply

A business that has crossed the statutory thresholds and needs someone accountable for keeping it clean.

Every statutory obligation met, evidenced and provable — with a named person answerable for it.

  • HR policy audit and drafting, written for your business
  • Labour law guidance across EPF, ESIC, Gratuity, Bonus, Maternity
  • POSH — policy, IC, annual return, awareness session
  • Compliance calendar plus an evidence log you can show an inspector
  • Full contract and letter set, onboarding and exit SOPs
  • Payroll processing support, statutory filings, named consultant

When to move on: when policy compliance is solved but the process still lives in the owner's head.

11 – 500 employees

Operate

An owner who has stopped knowing everyone's name, and is now the bottleneck in every people decision.

HR runs on a system instead of on you. Leave, attendance, payroll and hiring happen without your involvement.

  • Everything in Comply
  • Alvora HRMS core — employee master, leave, attendance, shifts, payroll, documents
  • Employee self-service on web and mobile
  • Org structure, grade framework and role definitions
  • Workforce plan, headcount forecast, department KPI framework
  • Recruitment and background verification at member rates

When to move on: when the system runs but you still cannot tell who is performing.

21 – 500 employees

Perform

A business where the managers are inconsistent, ratings mean nothing, and good people have started leaving.

You can see who is performing and prove why. Reviews are evidence-linked, calibrated and defensible.

  • Everything in Operate
  • Alvora HRMS Goals & Performance — cascaded KPIs, evidence-linked reviews
  • Competency framework with behavioural rating anchors
  • Review cycle and calibration facilitation, with documented rationale
  • Engagement pulse, eNPS and action tracking
  • Attrition analysis, exit intelligence and stay interviews

When to move on: when performance is visible but pay does not follow it.

41 – 500 employees

Partner

A business where pay decisions are becoming grievances and the owner needs the whole loop closed.

Every people decision is defensible, and the improvement is provable against a locked baseline.

  • Everything in Perform
  • Alvora HRMS Compensation — merit cycles, budgets, policy checks, letters
  • Alvora Hire — every source, zero duplicates, evidence-based shortlists
  • Salary banding, compa-ratio, merit guideline matrix
  • Pay equity analysis and remediation plan
  • People analytics, annual re-measurement, quarterly business review

When to move on: when the client builds their own HR team, move them to Counsel rather than losing them.

251+ employees

Counsel

An organisation with its own HR function that is running, but is not yet telling leadership anything it can act on.

Your HR team, made sharper. We advise, benchmark and prove — we do not run it for you.

  • Retained senior advisor — strategy, org design, escalations
  • Alvora HRMS at scale, licensed to your team with admin enablement
  • Annual Lens diagnostic and annual Proof re-measurement
  • Organisation design and operating-model advisory
  • Executive search at preferential rates, leadership development
  • Benchmarking against your own history, not borrowed industry figures

The end of the ladder. From here the relationship deepens rather than escalates.

Every service above traces to one of the 17 Growth Loop programmes or the three Alvora platforms. Each pack is scoped to your headcount, and the delivery quantities behind it — sessions per month, on-site days, review cycles — are confirmed in writing before contracting. Fees are quoted against that scope.
