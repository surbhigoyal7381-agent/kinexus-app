What it looks like

Numbers from real operations.

We learned this work where the margins are thin, the data is messy, and nobody has patience for software theatre. Every figure below is measured against where that business started — not an industry average, not a vendor benchmark.

12% → 7%
Delivery exceptions · logistics operator

Consignments went out on trust and came back as complaints — roughly one in seven arriving late, short, or at the wrong door. Nobody could tell you which, until the customer did. We replaced ad-hoc spot-checks with one systematic check-and-log step at dispatch. Twelve weeks later exceptions had almost halved, and the claims and re-delivery bill fell with them. No AI required.

+30%
Operational efficiency · steel manufacturer

Performance was a conversation that happened once a year, and the numbers behind it lived in a supervisor's head. We put in continuous performance management the whole floor could see — output, quality and downtime by shift, visible to the person doing the work, not just the person reviewing it. Transparency did the rest: when people trust how they're measured, they fix things before anyone asks. That's where the 30% came from.
