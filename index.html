The Reality

Your spreadsheets are quietly eating your margin

You've already done the hard part—you make a good product and you've found buyers. The leak happens in the gap between those two things, and most owners don't realize how much it's costing until they see it written down.

📋 Hours lost to data

Your team spends 2–3 hours daily compiling reports, chasing emails, reconciling spreadsheets. That's 500+ hours a year—roughly a quarter of a full-time role spent on work a system should be doing.

🔍 Defects reach buyers

Spot-checking misses flaws. Buyers reject shipments. Each rejection can wipe out the margin on the entire shipment—and damage trust that took years to build. You're betting the relationship on luck.

💰 Working capital's stuck

Manual chasing means 50+ day payment cycles. Every extra 10 days your receivables sit unpaid, cash worth roughly 3% of your annual turnover stays locked away—and you pay interest to borrow it back.

📞 Suppliers delay, you suffer

WhatsApp follow-ups. No visibility into delays until production stalls. Each delay means premium freight, overtime, and idle machines—costs that repeat monthly and rarely show up in any report.

📄 Export docs take forever

HS codes, certificates, GST compliance—all hand-compiled. 4–6 hours per shipment. One error can hold a shipment at customs for days—penalties, demurrage, and a nervous buyer.

🎯 Flying blind

You don't know real-time output, true rejection rates, or profit by order. No data means no strategy. You're scaling risk, not opportunity.

Questions

The questions every owner asks us first

Do I have to replace Tally or my existing software? +
No. We build on top of what you already use. Tally, Excel, WhatsApp, your machines—we integrate with all of it. Zero disruption to daily operations. Rip-and-replace is the opposite of what we do.
Will my business be run by AI from day one? +
No. Phase 1 is digitization and data visibility—that alone saves huge costs. AI automation is layered in step by step, on your real data, only once it's proven. Anything irreversible stays under your approval.
What if I'm not exporting yet? +
Perfect. Most clients start domestic. The Digital Backbone strengthens your operation immediately—cuts manual work, frees working capital, improves visibility. Export becomes optional, not a prerequisite. When the buyer comes, you're ready.
Will my team resist the change? +
Only if it's done badly—and the whole approach is designed around not doing it badly. Nothing gets replaced, so nobody's expertise becomes obsolete. Changes come one at a time, at your team's pace, with hands-on vernacular training. And we deliberately start with the work people hate most, so the system's first impression is "it took away my worst task," not "it's watching me."
How does pricing work? +
Fixed scope, agreed before we start—no open-ended billing. Each engagement is priced around the bottleneck you choose, with milestones tied to a dashboard you can check anytime. The guarantee applies throughout: no measurable progress by Week 12, we extend at no cost. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll give you a straight number for your scope.
How do you find manufacturers to work with? +
Systematic sourcing: industry clustering, official registries, trade associations, cluster partnerships. Home base is North India—Ludhiana, Dera Bassi, Baddi, Ambala, Kanpur—now extending the same cluster-first approach to other developing markets. That's what lets us deliver deep expertise at MSME prices.
You started in North India. Who do you serve now? +
Manufacturing MSMEs in India and across developing markets—same segments, same problems, same playbook. What we don't chase is global enterprise; the big consultancies own that game. Wherever we work, the model stays cluster-first and depth-first. North India is our proving ground; the playbook it produced is what we bring everywhere else.
How do you measure ROI? Can I trust the numbers? +
We baseline before we start, then track everything on a shared dashboard: cash released, rejections cut, units produced, hours saved—each tied to financial impact, reviewed with you weekly. No measurable progress by Week 12? We extend at no cost.
