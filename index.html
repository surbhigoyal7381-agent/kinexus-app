01 — The window, and the wall

The door to global markets is open. Most can't walk through it.

From 15 July 2026 the UK drops duties on 99% of Indian exports; the EU deal is signed and the UAE's already doubled trade. But here's the trap: only about 28% of India's eligible exports actually claim their FTA benefit — because the rules-of-origin paperwork can cost more than the duty it saves. The tariff falls; the paperwork eats the margin.

Stall 01 · Getting ready

"Not export ready"

Spreadsheets and WhatsApp, no batch traceability, quality that varies between batches — and a buyer's audit that ends the conversation.

Stall 02 · First buyer

Can't win the buyer

Slow quotations, leads lost in the noise, and no credibility when an overseas buyer runs due diligence on your operation.

Stall 03 · Ship & clear

Shipment held up

Missing or inconsistent documents, destination rules misunderstood — the single biggest cause of rejected export shipments.

The FTA tariff advantage is temporary. The lasting edge goes to whoever becomes operationally export-ready first.

Five ways the export dream leaks away

Most Indian exporters don't fail at making the product or finding the buyer. They fail at the operational chaos in between — and the research backs it up. Here's where the money, time and confidence leak out, and who in our alliance plugs each leak.

LEAK 03

Wrong HS code & compliance

One wrong classification can swing landed cost 8–12% — enough to turn a profitable order into a loss. Document errors are the #1 cause of customs delays.

⬡ Execution
LEAK 04

Failing the buyer audit

No buyer validation, and no traceability to survive due diligence. Shipments are rejected on quality, specs or paperwork — not price.

◆ Strategy⬡ Execution
LEAK 05

Chaos & agent dependence

Docs, logistics, payments and compliance run on spreadsheets, WhatsApp and middlemen — blocking working capital and visibility.
