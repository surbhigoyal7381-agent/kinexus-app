What we do

Two pieces of work. Most businesses need both.

One builds the system you never had. The other makes the systems you already paid for do the work by themselves. Start with whichever is costing you more.

Service 01

Build the system that isn't there

Custom digital solutions for the parts of your business still running on spreadsheets, paper and memory. We implement a proven enterprise platform and shape it around how your business actually works — your process, your terms, your approvals — instead of forcing your team into someone else's template.

  • Production planning, job cards and shop-floor tracking
  • Inventory, batches, stores and material movement
  • Purchase, vendors and approvals with a real audit trail
  • Sales orders through to dispatch and invoicing
  • Quality checks recorded where the work happens
  • Job costing you can actually trust
  • Maintenance, compliance and document control
  • Role-based dashboards — the owner's view, the supervisor's view

First working module live in weeks, not quarters. Built with your team, then handed over to them.

Service 02

Agentic AI workflows on what you already run

You've already paid for an ERP, Tally, a CRM, shared drives and a dozen WhatsApp groups. We build AI agents that work across them — reading, chasing, reconciling, drafting and escalating — so the routine work happens without a person driving it, and a human steps in only where judgement is needed.

  • Enquiries and orders read straight out of email, PDF and WhatsApp
  • Purchase orders and invoices matched, flagged and queued
  • Follow-ups and chasers that run themselves
  • Documents extracted, checked and filed against the right record
  • Exceptions escalated with the evidence already attached
  • Reports and MIS drafted before the meeting, not after it
  • Approvals routed to the person who can actually decide
  • Every agent action logged, reversible and reviewable

No rip-and-replace. The agents sit on top of your existing systems, which stay exactly where they are.

What it looks like

Numbers from real operations.

We learned this work where the margins are thin, the data is messy, and nobody has patience for software theatre. Every figure below is measured against where that business started — not an industry average, not a vendor benchmark.

12% → 7%
Delivery exceptions · logistics operator

Consignments went out on trust and came back as complaints — roughly one in seven arriving late, short, or at the wrong door. Nobody could tell you which, until the customer did. We replaced ad-hoc spot-checks with one systematic check-and-log step at dispatch. Twelve weeks later exceptions had almost halved, and the claims and re-delivery bill fell with them. No AI required.

+30%
Operational efficiency · steel manufacturer

Performance was a conversation that happened once a year, and the numbers behind it lived in a supervisor's head. We put in continuous performance management the whole floor could see — output, quality and downtime by shift, visible to the person doing the work, not just the person reviewing it. Transparency did the rest: when people trust how they're measured, they fix things before anyone asks. That's where the 30% came from.
