The Real Cost of Standing Still

Every Day Without Systems Is Money Left on the Floor

These are not hypothetical problems. They are the daily reality of 85% of manufacturing MSMEs in the Chandigarh–Ludhiana–Ambala belt.

⚙️

Unplanned Machine Downtime

No sensors, no alerts — breakdowns discovered only when production stops. Average: 2–3 hours of lost production daily in un-monitored plants.

📦

Inventory Mismatches

Raw material, WIP, and finished goods tracked in three different Excel files — if at all. Stockouts and excess stock exist simultaneously on the same shopfloor.

❌

Quality Rejections from Buyers

2–8% of revenue eroded by defects caught at the buyer's end — not yours. OEM relationships at risk with every batch dispatch.

📱

WhatsApp as Your ERP

Production updates, shift reports, purchase requests, and delivery challans — all buried in a WhatsApp group. No traceability, no accountability.

💰

Cash Flow Gaps from Delayed Receivables

No automated follow-up on outstanding invoices. Owners manually chasing buyers. Working capital trapped in receivables, borrowed at 14–18% to bridge the gap.

👤

Owner as the System

The business cannot run without the owner making 50 decisions a day. No dashboards, no alerts, no delegation possible. Scale is impossible.

Stage-Matched Solutions

The Right Intervention at the Right Stage

No generic software packages. Every sprint is matched to your current stage — standard where possible, customised where it matters.

Stage: CRAWL

First Light — Make the Invisible Visible

At the CRAWL stage, the single most valuable thing we can do is capture what's actually happening. You can't improve what you can't see. Sprint 1 focuses entirely on data visibility with the least possible disruption to your current operations.

Our philosophy: Use what your team already uses (WhatsApp), add the minimum hardware (sensors where needed), and create one source of truth. No expensive ERP. No long training. Results in 6 weeks.

  • Daily production summary on owner's phone by 7 AM, automatically generated
  • Machine uptime tracked without touching the machine (clamp-on sensors)
  • Purchase requests, delivery challans structured out of WhatsApp chaos
  • Attendance and shift data captured digitally — no more paper registers
  • One dashboard the owner can check in 2 minutes
⚡ Machine Counter Sensors Standard — Plug & Play

Non-invasive clamp-on or optical sensors attached to any existing machine — no rewiring, no downtime. Sends production count data to your phone and a central dashboard in real time.

Deploy: 1–2 days No machine modification Any legacy machine
📱 WhatsApp Production Bot Standard — Plug & Play

Supervisors send structured updates to a WhatsApp bot (production qty, rejects, downtime reason). Bot auto-compiles into a daily report and sends to owner at shift end. Zero learning curve for workers.

Deploy: 3–5 days Works on any phone No app install needed
🏭 Digital Shift Report Standard — Plug & Play

Replaces the paper register completely. Supervisors fill a 2-minute digital form on a tablet at end of shift. Owner gets a structured PDF and dashboard summary automatically.

Deploy: 1 week Hindi/Punjabi UI option Offline capable
📋 Purchase Request Workflow Customisable

Structured purchase request → approval → PO issuance workflow replacing WhatsApp messages. Configured to your approval hierarchy, supplier list, and Tally integration.

Deploy: 2–3 weeks Tally-integrated Mobile-first
Stage: WALK

Connect the Dots — One Integrated View

At the WALK stage you have data scattered across systems — Tally, Excel, WhatsApp. The problem isn't that data doesn't exist; it's that no one trusts any of it because it lives in 3 places with 3 different versions. Sprint focus: integration and a single source of truth.

Our philosophy: We don't replace your existing Tally or ERP. We build a lightweight integration layer on top that pulls data together and adds the agentic workflows your current system was never designed to do.

  • Inventory positions updated automatically from purchase and production data
  • Supplier follow-up automated — no more manual calls about delayed deliveries
  • Customer invoice ageing monitored with automatic escalation alerts
  • Production plan vs actual visible on one screen, updated hourly
  • Reorder alerts triggered automatically when stock hits minimum levels
🔗 Tally + WhatsApp Integration Bridge Standard — Plug & Play

Pulls sales, purchase, and inventory data from your existing TallyPrime automatically. Sends daily P&L snapshot, outstanding receivables, and stock alerts to owner on WhatsApp every morning.

Deploy: 1 week No Tally changes needed Works with Tally 9+
🤖 Supplier Follow-Up Agent Standard — Plug & Play

Automated WhatsApp/SMS messages sent to suppliers when delivery dates pass without confirmation. Escalates to phone call list if no response in 24 hours. Tracks and logs all follow-ups automatically.

Deploy: 5–7 days 24/7 automated Full audit trail
📊 Live Inventory Dashboard Customisable

Single-screen inventory view across raw material, WIP, and finished goods — updated in real time from production and dispatch data. Configurable for your specific material codes and warehouse layout.

Deploy: 2–3 weeks Barcode / QR optional Multi-location

Stage: RUN

From Data to Decisions — Make the System Smarter

At the RUN stage your systems are connected but your people are still doing the analysis. Sprint focus: layer AI-driven intelligence on top of existing connected data so the system surfaces insights and recommendations — not just numbers.

Our philosophy: We don't rebuild your ERP. We build an intelligence layer on top: AI agents that monitor your data continuously, surface anomalies, and take pre-approved actions automatically.

  • Quality rejection rates reduced by 30–50% through automated pre-dispatch checks
  • Predictive maintenance alerts 24–48 hours before equipment failure
  • Production scheduling optimised automatically based on orders and capacity
  • Energy consumption monitored with anomaly detection
  • Compliance documentation auto-generated for ISO / GMP audits
🔍 AI Quality Inspection Assistant Customisable

Computer vision or structured data AI that flags potential quality deviations before dispatch. Trained on your specific defect types and buyer rejection patterns. Reduces outward rejections.

Deploy: 4–6 weeks Vision or data-driven Buyer-specific rules

Deploy: 4–5 weeks Per-machine calibration SMS / WhatsApp alerts
📅 AI Production Scheduler Customisable

Generates optimised daily/weekly production schedules based on order priorities, material availability, machine capacity, and workforce. Reduces idle time and late deliveries simultaneously.

Deploy: 4–6 weeks ERP-integrated Daily plan on WhatsApp
📑 Auto Compliance Documentation Customisable

Deploy: 3–5 weeks ISO / WHO-GMP ready Audit trail built-in
Stage: FLY

Autonomous Operations — Your Factory Thinks for Itself

At the FLY stage, Kinexus deploys full agentic workflow orchestration. AI agents take pre-approved decisions autonomously: reordering raw materials, rescheduling production, following up on payments, and escalating anomalies — with the owner reviewing outcomes, not making every call.

Our philosophy: The owner shifts from firefighter to strategist. The plant runs on intelligence, not heroics. Every workflow that was previously owner-dependent is now system-driven with human oversight on exceptions only.

  • Fully autonomous procurement within pre-approved parameters — zero manual POs below threshold
  • Customer demand signals used to automatically adjust production schedules
  • AI agents negotiating delivery dates with suppliers based on production requirements
  • Real-time profitability by SKU, order, and customer — always live
  • Export documentation, compliance certificates, and customs workflows automated end-to-end
🧠 Autonomous Procurement Agent Fully Customised

AI agent monitors inventory levels, production requirements, and lead times. Autonomously raises purchase orders within approved limits, selects suppliers, and confirms delivery dates — with exception-only owner review.

Deploy: Sprint 5–6 Zero manual POs under threshold
📈 Demand-Driven Production Orchestrator Fully Customised

Reads buyer order patterns and external demand signals to continuously optimise production mix, sequencing, and capacity utilisation. Outputs daily plan automatically to shopfloor supervisors.

Deploy: Sprint 5–7 Multi-buyer orchestration

Deploy: Sprint 4–5 Natural language queries
🌐 Export & Compliance Automation Fully Customised

Deploy: Sprint 5–6 WHO-GMP / ISO / DGFT
