What "evidence-first" means in practice

Evidence inA CV, an interview note, a goal check-in, a payroll record. Captured once, at the point it happens.
Decision outA shortlist, a rating, an increment. Every one of them links back to what it was based on.
Outcome measuredDid the hire stay? Did the rating predict anything? The platform keeps score.
Evidence back inThe measurement becomes the next input, so the system gets better at the thing it does.

Why it is built this way

Consulting and software are usually two companies pretending to be one.

Here they are the same business read twice. The loop the AllAboutHR services run is the same loop the platform runs — evidence in, decision out, outcome measured, evidence back in. AllAboutHR Lens diagnoses it, Calibrate designs the framework, Alvora instruments it, and Proof re-measures at twelve months against a locked baseline.

That is why a rating in Alvora is not an opinion typed into a box. It is a conclusion with a trail behind it — which is the only kind that survives a grievance, an audit, or a question from the board.
