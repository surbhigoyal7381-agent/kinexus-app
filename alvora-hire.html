Ask in plain English

Stop writing boolean strings. Just say what you want.

"Senior Java developers in Bangalore who've scaled Series B fintechs." Type it the way you'd say it to a colleague. Alvora Hire reads meaning, not keywords — so you find the person who fits, not the person who happened to use your exact words on their CV.

DPDP compliance, built in

Candidate data is somebody's life. Treat it that way.

India's DPDP Act applies to you whether or not you've read it. Alvora Hire ships with consent management and data-retention enforcement built in — not as a paid add-on, not as a PDF policy you're supposed to follow manually. It's the part nobody asks about in the demo and everybody worries about at 2am.
