Where We See Pharmaceuticals Manufacturers Stuck

The operational gaps quietly eating your margins

Pharma MSMEs in this cluster face a unique mix of GMP rigor and manual, paper-driven processes that slow everything down.

??

Paper Batch Manufacturing Records

BMRs and BPRs are still filled by hand, making review, retrieval and audit preparation a multi-day scramble before every inspection.

??

Disconnected QC & QA Data

Lab test results live in one register, deviation logs in another, and CAPA tracking in a third — with no single view of a batch's full quality story.

???

Manual Temperature & Storage Logging

Cold-chain and storage condition logs are recorded by hand on rounds, risking gaps that become major audit observations.

??

Vendor & Raw Material Qualification Tracking

Approved vendor lists, COAs and material qualification status are tracked in folders, making it hard to confirm compliance status at a glance.

??

Slow Regulatory Submission Prep

Compiling documentation for product registrations or export dossiers means weeks of manually assembling files from multiple departments.

??

No Real-Time Production-to-Quality Linkage

Production schedules and QC release status aren't connected, so finished goods often sit waiting for paperwork rather than actual quality issues.

Digital Batch Record & Documentation System Standard Module

Convert key batch manufacturing and packing records into structured digital forms with built-in checks, timestamps and electronic sign-offs.

  • Audit prep time reduced from days to hours
  • Built-in completeness checks reduce observations
  • Searchable digital archive of all batch history
Quality Data Control Tower Standard Module

A unified dashboard linking QC results, deviations, CAPAs and vendor qualification status so QA teams see the complete quality picture for any batch or material.

  • Single source of truth for quality status
  • Faster CAPA closure tracking
  • Vendor compliance status visible at a glance
Agentic Regulatory Documentation Assistant Custom Build

An AI agent that assembles export and registration dossiers by pulling from your digital batch records, COAs and specifications — producing a structured first draft for QA review.

  • Dossier prep time cut significantly
  • Fewer last-minute missing-document delays
  • Consistent formatting across submissions
Production-to-Release Tracking Custom Build

Connects production scheduling with QC release status so finished goods movement reflects real quality clearance, not guesswork.

  • Reduced finished-goods dwell time
