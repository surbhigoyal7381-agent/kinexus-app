Where We See Bicycle & Auto Parts Manufacturers Stuck

The operational gaps quietly eating your margins

These are the issues we hear in almost every conversation with component manufacturers in this cluster — often described as 'just how the industry works'.

??

SKU Sprawl in Spreadsheets

Hundreds of part variants tracked across disconnected Excel files, leading to stock-outs on fast movers and dead capital tied up in slow ones.

??

Quote Turnaround on the Phone

Costing for new OEM/aftermarket orders depends on one or two people doing mental math — quotes take days and margins get guessed, not calculated.

??

Manual Quality Checks

Inward and outward QC is paper-based, making it hard to trace a defect batch back to a specific shift, machine or vendor lot.

??

Vendor & Job-Work Coordination

Plating, forging and machining job-work is tracked on WhatsApp and call logs, causing delays that cascade into missed dispatch dates.

??

Export Documentation Bottlenecks

Invoices, packing lists, COOs and shipping bills are prepared manually each time, slowing down container loading and increasing error-driven rejections at customs.

??

No Real-Time Production Visibility

Owners only learn about a delayed batch when the customer calls — there's no dashboard showing where every order actually stands on the floor.

Built for Bicycle & Auto Parts

A Crawl-Walk-Run-Fly path tailored to your shop floor

We typically start where the pain is sharpest — usually costing and SKU visibility — and layer in automation as confidence builds.

Digital Costing & Quotation Engine Standard Module

A structured costing sheet connected to live material rates and machine-hour costs, so any team member can generate an accurate OEM quote in minutes, not days.

  • Quote turnaround cut from days to under an hour
  • Consistent margins across every quotation
  • Historical quote library for repeat orders
Inventory & SKU Control Tower Standard Module

A simple, centralized inventory system covering raw material, WIP and finished SKUs with reorder alerts — built to match how your store actually operates.

  • Stock-outs on fast-moving parts reduced
  • Slow-moving inventory identified and liquidated
  • One source of truth instead of five spreadsheets
Agentic Export Documentation Assistant Custom Build

An AI agent that auto-generates invoices, packing lists and draft shipping documents from your order and dispatch data, flagging missing compliance fields before they cause delays.

  • Documentation prep time cut by up to 70%
  • Fewer customs queries and rejections
  • Audit-ready paper trail for every shipment
Production & Job-Work Tracking Dashboard Custom Build

A live dashboard pulling status from your floor and job-work vendors, so management can see exactly where every order stands without phone calls.

  • End-to-end order visibility for owners and sales
  • Job-work delays caught early, not at dispatch
  • Foundation for future machine-level IoT integration
