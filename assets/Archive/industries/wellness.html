Why Wellness, Why Now

The FTA tailwind is real — but only if you can prove it

India's nutraceutical exports grew ~31% year-on-year, Ayush & herbal exports crossed US$689M (+5.9%), and the UK-India FTA is cutting cosmetics & personal-care duties from 20–30% toward 0–5%. The demand is rising fast — the constraint is documentation, traceability and proof of quality.

Where We See Wellness Manufacturers Stuck

The operational gaps that block export orders

Buyer and regulator expectations have outrun back-office systems — turning winnable export demand into rejected shipments and stalled approvals.

??

Manual GMP / AYUSH Records

Batch manufacturing records, GMP and AYUSH documentation live on paper and in scattered spreadsheets, making audits and renewals a scramble.

??

Quality & Stability Testing Logs

Raw-material, in-process and stability test results are hand-recorded, with gaps that fail to satisfy EU/UK/UAE buyer due diligence.

???

Label & Claim Compliance

Ingredient declarations, claims and label artwork must vary by destination market — managed manually, errors trigger customs rejections.

??

Batch-to-Source Traceability

Linking a finished supplement or oil back to herb/raw-material lots and suppliers is largely manual — or simply not possible on demand.

??

Export Certification Paperwork

COAs, free-sale certificates, ISO/GMP proofs and Rules-of-Origin documents are compiled by hand for every shipment, delaying dispatch.

??

D2C & Marketplace Channel Chaos

Orders from eCommerce, marketplaces and distributors arrive in silos, with no single view of demand, inventory or fulfilment status.
