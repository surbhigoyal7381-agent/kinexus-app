The Window

UK duties on Indian auto components: heading to zero

Under the India–UK CETA, in force from 15 July 2026, UK import duties of up to 18% on engineering goods and auto components drop to zero. Global buyers pursuing China+1 sourcing increasingly ask for digital proof—quality trends, traceability, delivery records—before they ask for price. The window favours the operationally ready.
