The Standard

Your next audit will be won or lost in your records

Most failed inspections aren't about bad medicine—they're about bad records of good medicine. Transcription gaps, backdated entries, a batch history that takes three days to reconstruct: none of it means your product was unsafe, and all of it reads as a data-integrity finding. The fix isn't a thicker SOP binder. It's capturing the data once, at source, so the record is simply a by-product of doing the work.
