Avoid these

Where scientific-instrument exporters most often leak margin

The specific traps that turn a promising scientific-instrument order into a delayed, rejected or loss-making one.

Missing conformity marking

No CE/UKCA (or device registration) means no access to regulated EU/UK markets.

No calibration traceability

Without traceable calibration and test records, buyers in serious markets won't trust the instrument.

Weak quality documentation

Gaps in ISO-grade quality records cost you premium buyers and repeat orders.
