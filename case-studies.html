Engineering & Auto Ancillaries
Precision components · Dera Bassi

A Dera Bassi precision unit, systematized.

Before

Jobs were scheduled by memory. Rush orders derailed the plan, and machine utilisation sat at 68% while everyone worked flat out.

What changed

Production scheduling was systematized — live job status and capacity-aware planning on the machines they already owned.

After

The same three CNC machines delivered 25% more output with no capital spend. On-time delivery rose from 78% to 94%, and that record unlocked 12 new OEM orders within nine months.

Hosiery & Textiles
Knitwear mill · Ludhiana

A Ludhiana mill, made auditable.

Before

Buyers were rejecting one in seven consignments. Quality lived in the checker's eye, and the register couldn't show a single pattern.

What changed

Systematic inspection workflows went in on the line — every 10th part checked and logged, so defects were caught and traced at the source.

After

The rejection rate fell from 12% to 7% within 12 weeks and rework costs fell sharply. The quality record that came out of it now answers a buyer's audit questions on the spot.

Pharma, Nutraceuticals & Wellness
Wellness brand · Baddi

A Baddi brand, unstuck.

Before

Customers took 52 days on average to pay. New orders couldn't scale because the cash to buy stock for them was stuck in receivables.

What changed

Systematic receivables discipline — follow-ups that happen on schedule and a DSO number someone owns — alongside two sales hires made with AllAboutHR.

After

DSO dropped to 34 days, releasing cash worth roughly 5% of annual turnover. That funded the inventory to take on larger accounts, and the order book has grown with the new sales team.
