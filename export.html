The Hard Truth

Why most factories miss the window

We've sat across the table from enough owners to know the pattern. It's almost never the product that fails—it's the proof. An international buyer asks five questions, and the answers live in your systems, not your head.

"Show me your rejection rate—by batch"

If quality data lives in a register or a supervisor's memory, this conversation ends politely and goes nowhere. Buyers want trends they can audit, not assurances.

"What's your on-time delivery record?"

Not "we're usually on time." A number, over twelve months, with the misses explained. Export orders run on shipping schedules that don't wait.

"Can you trace this lot to its raw material?"

Traceability is table stakes for UK and EU buyers. If a recall happens, they need the chain in hours—and so does your compliance paperwork.

"Send the compliance pack by Friday"

Certificates of origin, test reports, HS classifications. Hand-compiled, this takes days and invites errors. One wrong document holds a container at port.

"What's your landed cost at this volume?"

Quoting exports without knowing your true cost per unit—at volumes you've never run—is how factories win orders that lose money.

"Who do I talk to next week?"

Export relationships need a responsive counterpart with data at their fingertips. If every answer requires three phone calls, trust erodes fast.

The Agentic Roadmap

AI agents for the export value chain—built in phases, not promised overnight

Once your digital backbone is live and proven, we layer in agents along the export chain—each one deployed only when the data under it is solid. And a rule we won't bend: agents draft, humans approve. Anything compliance-critical goes through your team or your CHA before it leaves the building.

Quote
🔎 HS Code & FTA Eligibility Agent

Suggests HS classifications from your product specs, checks preferential duty eligibility under agreements like the India–UK CETA and India–UAE CEPA, and flags the rules-of-origin conditions you'd need to meet—before you commit to a price.

Quote
🧮 Landed Cost & Quotation Agent

Builds market-wise landed cost—freight, post-FTA duty, insurance—on top of your true production cost from the backbone, and drafts FOB/CIF quotations. No more winning orders that quietly lose money.

Quote
📨 RFQ Response Agent

When a buyer inquiry lands, the agent assembles spec sheets, certifications, and your actual quality history into a professional response—so you reply in hours while competitors take weeks.

Comply
🧾 Origin Evidence Agent

Compiles rules-of-origin evidence from your bill of materials and production records to support origin declarations under FTAs—organized, traceable, and ready for your CHA's review rather than rebuilt from scratch each shipment.

Comply
📡 Regulatory Watch Agent

Target markets change labeling rules, testing standards, and import requirements without sending you a memo. This agent watches the requirements attached to your HS codes and alerts you before a change strands a container.

Ship
📄 Document Pack Agent

Commercial invoice, packing list, origin declaration—drafted automatically from live production data instead of hand-compiled over days. Your team and CHA review and sign; the agent just kills the drudgery and the transcription errors.

Ship
🚢 Shipment & Buyer Comms Agent

Tracks container milestones, flags delays the moment they appear, and keeps your buyer informed proactively across time zones. Silence is how export relationships die; this agent makes sure there never is any.

Get Paid
💰 Export Receivables & LC Agent

Watches letter-of-credit document deadlines, chases export invoices systematically, and assembles the data for incentive claims like RoDTEP and duty drawback—organized for your consultant to file, so nothing gets left unclaimed.

These agents are on our phased development roadmap and are deployed engagement by engagement, only after your data backbone has proven itself. Compliance-critical outputs—classifications, declarations, filings—are always drafts for human review, never auto-submitted.
