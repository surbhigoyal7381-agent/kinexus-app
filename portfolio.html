Featured case study

A credit economy, run by agents.

WISE Agentic Infrastructure

Eight agents, ten thousand entrepreneurs.

WISE is a credit-based AI economy built for 10,000 women entrepreneurs — the kind of user who has a phone, a business, and no patience for a dashboard. Instead of one general assistant trying to do everything badly, the system runs eight specialists: Sakhi handles onboarding in the user's own language, Prerna carries her through growth, and six others take the work behind them.

Credits meter what each agent does, so the economics stay visible instead of disappearing into an API bill. The architecture is the point: specialists that hand off cleanly beat one model asked to be everything.

The journey

Where the habits came from.

Surbhi Goyal
Founder, Kinexus
Full profile on LinkedIn →

Twenty-two years, and the same job the whole way through: make the software actually get used. I started writing automation for enterprise BPM suites, moved into scrum and performance engineering on a Nordic HR platform, then spent seven years running product operations for it — growing the India organization from one person to 150+ with attrition under 2%, and owning the SOC 2 and ISO 27001 work that comes with holding other people's employee data.

Most recently VP of Products, taking AI-native platforms from idea to enterprise deployment — a GenAI document assistant in production, and a data lakehouse teams stand up in under a month. That's where the conviction hardened: AI belongs only where it earns its place, and everywhere else a simple system wins.

Every role taught the same lesson from a different angle: adoption is the deliverable, not installation.
